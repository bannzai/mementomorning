Memento Morning is an alarm clock you stop by answering one question every morning.

There is only one question.
"If today were your last day, what would you want to do?"

■ A morning ritual
- Your alarm rings at the time you set
- Answer today's question, and your morning begins
- Until you answer, the alarm quietly keeps calling you back

■ A journal of your life
- One answer a day, quietly accumulating
- Your life calendar fills in like drops of ink, one morning at a time
- On the seventh morning, your seven answers appear together for the first time

■ An evening check-in
- At night, we ask only one thing: "Did you keep today's answer?"
- The days you did and the days you didn't both remain as they are

■ Premium
- Endless follow-up alarms: it keeps ringing until you answer, so you never skip a morning
- Every morning you have answered stays, beyond the last 7 days
- Night reminders at the times you choose, up to three a night

■ Privacy
- Your answers are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to a server

No confetti, no badges, no streaks on fire.
Just a quiet question, and your answers.
