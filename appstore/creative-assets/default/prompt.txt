Idea: start the day with a quiet question. Subject, SMALL, in the exact center: a small half-circle rising sun in dawn glow color #C2A183 giving off a restrained soft light, and in front of it a plain open paper booklet in warm white #E9E7E2, wide and thin, a few pages and a center fold, pages completely blank; a clear gap between sun and booklet.
